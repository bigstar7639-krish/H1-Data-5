## 1️⃣ Philosophy எதுக்கு? (What’s it for?)

Philosophy basically means **“love of wisdom”** (Greek: *philosophia*).  
- **Why are we here?** – existence, purpose, meaning.  
- **How do we know stuff?** – knowledge, truth, belief.  
- **What’s right or wrong?** – ethics, morality.  
- **What’s real?** – reality, mind‑body, space‑time.

In short, philosophy is the *big‑question‑machine* that tries to give a systematic answer to everything we wonder about.
